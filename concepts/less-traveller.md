# LeSS Traveller

A coordination practice from Large-Scale Scrum (Larman & Vodde): a scarce expert joins a different team each Sprint, coaching via pairing, workshops, and teaching. The goal is not to keep the expert as the go-to person — it is to spread their knowledge across teams until the expert is no longer the bottleneck.

---

## Connection to our work

The opposite pole from [[wegner-transactive-memory]]: TMS stabilizes on "ask the specialist," the Traveller actively dismantles that dependency by moving the knowledge, not just the answer. Relevant to [[mass-learning]] as the target state — mass-learning succeeds when the specialist becomes redundant, not when everyone knows whom to ask.
