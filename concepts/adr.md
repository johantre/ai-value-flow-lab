# ADR

Architecture Decision Record — a short, durable record of a single architectural decision: Title, Context, Decision, Status, Consequences (Nygard, 2011). Written "as a conversation with a future developer," it captures not just what was decided but why, so a later reader can evaluate the decision instead of blindly accepting or blindly reversing it.

---

## Connection to our work

The memory half of the [[raymond-the-cathedral-and-the-bazaar]] model: an [[rfc]] activates open discussion on an idea, an ADR is what survives that discussion. Without an ADR, reasoning collapses into a [[wegner-transactive-memory]]-style directory — "the decision is known, but only by asking whoever was in the room."
