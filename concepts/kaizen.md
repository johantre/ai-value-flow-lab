# Kaizen

Continuous, incremental improvement — traditionally associated with Toyota. Often misapplied as a one-off "kaizen event" (a week spent fixing one problem); Rother's research shows the real mechanism is a repeatable improvement *routine* practiced habitually, not an isolated intervention.
