# LeSS

Large-Scale Scrum (Larman & Vodde) — Scrum applied to multiple teams working on one product. Its core principle is "more with LeSS": scale through constraint (more responsible, customer-focused teams) rather than through extra roles, artifacts, or process layers.

---

## Connection to our work

The framework that [[less-traveller]] is a coordination practice within: LeSS deliberately avoids adding a management layer to spread knowledge across teams, and uses lightweight mechanisms like the Traveller instead.
