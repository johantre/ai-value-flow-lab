# Gemba

Japanese for "the real place" — go and observe where the work actually happens, rather than relying on reports or dashboards. Ohno's chalk circle (standing on the shop floor for hours to watch) is the classic illustration: understanding of work cannot be delegated to a representation of it.

## Connection to our work

Step ❷ of Rother's Improvement Kata (see [[rother-toyota-kata]]) — every cycle starts by establishing the current condition through direct observation, not assumption. Gemba is what keeps a post-mortem honest: the Notion page is a representation of the incident, not the incident itself.
